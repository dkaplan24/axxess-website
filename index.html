FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

Does this change how I pay my drivers?

No. Everything is handled automatically. Your payroll process stays exactly the same.

What happens if a van breaks down?

Your driver contacts Axxess directly. We handle maintenance and roadside. You're not involved.

Do my drivers need a credit card or deposit?

No. Payment comes out of their weekly earnings automatically. Nothing from their pocket.

What if a driver doesn't work one week?

If there's nothing earned, there's nothing deducted. Payment is tied entirely to active earnings.

What kind of van is it?

A Ford E-Transit — a new electric cargo van purpose-built for delivery routes.

Where are your depots?

Currently the Bronx, Queens, and Brooklyn. More locations coming.
